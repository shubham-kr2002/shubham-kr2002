## `whoami`

I'm Shubham — a **builder, AI engineer, systems thinker and founder**.

I spend an unreasonable amount of time thinking about **how things work, why they work, and whether they could work fundamentally better.**

I build across the boundary between:

```text
Artificial Intelligence
        ×
Software Engineering
        ×
Product
        ×
Design
        ×
Entrepreneurship
```

I'm less interested in collecting technologies and more interested in **what those technologies make possible**.

## 🤖 AI

I'm fascinated by AI not because it is fashionable, but because it changes the boundary of what a single person can build.

I explore:

* **LLM systems**
* **Agentic workflows**
* **RAG & retrieval**
* **Long-term memory**
* **Semantic search**
* **AI orchestration**
* **Tool-using agents**
* **AI coding systems**
* **Human–AI interfaces**

My interest isn't:

> *“How do I call an LLM?”*

It's:

> **“What becomes possible when intelligence becomes programmable?”**

---

## 🏗️ What I'm building

I tend to build at the intersection of **AI + real-world problems**.

### 🏥 Healthcare AI

Building AI-native systems that can understand fragmented medical information, reason over patient context, and reduce the amount of repetitive work humans have to do.

The goal isn't to replace experts.

**AI assists. Humans decide.**

### 🧠 AI Systems

Exploring memory, agents, orchestration, retrieval, tools, and the infrastructure required to make AI systems actually useful outside of demos.

### ⚙️ Developer Tools

I like tools that make developers dramatically more capable.

Especially systems that turn AI from a chatbot into an **actual engineering partner**.

## 🚀 Ambition

I don't want to optimize my life around becoming **slightly better at the things everyone else is already doing.**

I want to work on problems where the answer isn't obvious.

I want to build products that feel **impossible before they exist and obvious after they do.**

I'm still early.

I have a lot to learn.

A lot of things I'm building will fail.

That's fine.

The objective isn't to look successful.

**The objective is to become capable enough to build something genuinely consequential.**
