## 🧠 About Me

I'm **Shubham**, a builder interested in the intersection of **AI, software, systems, product, and entrepreneurship**.

I enjoy taking problems that feel unnecessarily complicated, breaking them down to their fundamentals, and building systems that make them feel simple.

I'm particularly interested in **AI agents, RAG, intelligent automation, developer tools, healthcare AI, and AI-native products.**

I don't want to just integrate AI into existing software.

**I want to rethink what software can become when intelligence is built into it.**

---

## ⚡ How I Think

I tend to question things before accepting them.

**Why does it work this way?**
**Is the assumption actually true?**
**What is the simplest underlying system?**
**Can this be built better?**

My biases:

> **Truth over comfort.**
> **First principles over convention.**
> **Depth over noise.**
> **Building over talking.**

I believe good engineering isn't about creating more complexity.

**It's about understanding complexity well enough to remove it.**

---

## 🏗️ What I'm Building

My projects usually live somewhere between **AI and real-world problems**.

* 🏥 **Healthcare AI** — systems that help people and professionals understand medical information and workflows.
* 🤖 **AI Agents** — tools that can reason, use tools, remember context, and actually get work done.
* 🧠 **Knowledge Systems** — RAG, semantic search, memory, and retrieval infrastructure.
* ⚙️ **Developer Tools** — making engineers dramatically more capable with AI.
* 🚀 **Products** — turning technical ideas into things people can actually use.

Some ideas work.

Some don't.

I keep building.
